## Verified concise product facts for final archive

The official 永楽堂 company page states: Akashi-caught octopus; plates at 170°C; pressure and gradual thin baking; cheese in the dough; face-larger finished size. The official product detail gives **package** dimensions 39.5 × 30.5 cm (not cracker diameter), and milk/soy allergen labeling. No exact cracker diameter was verified, so do not invent one or label the package width as a diameter. No one-ton pressure or two-minute timing was verified for this maker.

Recommended factual caption: `明石ぺったん焼 真だこ｜永楽堂` and `明石産の真だこを170°Cの鉄板で薄く焼いたせんべい。生地にチーズを使用。` Link the official product and company pages above. The interactive result may use a clearly identified photographic collage; the separate official archive photo should remain unchanged.
